Meet the Chef

Made by a professional chef.

Chef Thara Bakes is the home kitchen of Chef Thara Pfano Mahandana — an internationally experienced professional chef based in Thohoyandou, Limpopo.

Classically trained at the Limpopo Chefs Academy, Chef Thara has built her career in some of the finest kitchens in South Africa and the United States — from 5-star safari lodges to boutique hotels and premium fine-dining establishments.

Today she brings that same discipline, precision and professional eye to every dish that leaves her kitchen — whether it's a freshly baked treat, a comforting homemade meal or a celebration platter. Real ingredients. Small batches. Proper technique. And a genuine love for making people smile with the first bite.

From her kitchen in Thohoyandou, she delivers around the region — to homes, offices and celebrations.
